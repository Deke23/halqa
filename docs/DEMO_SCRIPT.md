# Halqa: demonstration video script (5 minutes at most)

**Status: run end to end by an independent tester on 20 November against the single-container build, then corrected.** Beats 1.1 to 1.8, 2.1 to 2.6 and 3.1 to 3.4 were each executed with the clicks written here. Defects that run found were fixed afterwards (the closed-case owner line, the urgent case header, the red-flag reply by text message, toast position), and the lines below describe the fixed behaviour. A first recording was made from this script on 20 November, and beats 1.4, 2.4, 3.3 and 3.4 were then corrected to match what it showed. The submitted video is a shorter cut of about two minutes: it keeps beats 1.1 to 1.7, 2.1, 2.2, 2.4, 2.6, 3.1, 3.2 and 3.4 and the transparency page, drops the e-consult and the weekly action, and uses a shorter narration read by a synthetic voice. Where a name or an order differs on screen, trust the screen and fix this file.

Everything shown is synthetic and simulated. Do not say or imply that the system has been clinically validated, that it improves outcomes, or that it is connected to any real system.

Running time: opening 0:15, part 1 1:45, part 2 1:50, part 3 0:50, closing 0:15. Total 4:55.

## Pre-flight checklist

1. Start the API with demo mode on (the default). Check `http://localhost:8000/api/health` answers.
2. Open the app at the landing page. Click **Reset demo data** (it sits beside the heading "Choose who to sign in as") and confirm with the **Reset demo data** button in the dialog. Wait for the toast "Demo data was reset."
3. Do not move the demo clock before filming. The seeded overdue case is already overdue after a reset.
4. Browser: Chrome or Edge, window about 1280 by 720, zoom 100 percent, one tab, no bookmarks bar. The patient screens sit inside a phone-like frame (maximum width 480), so they stay readable at this size. Close other notifications.
5. Language: do not set it by hand. Each persona signs in with its own language: Khalid in Arabic, Jean-Paul in French, staff in English. Part 1 switches Khalid to English with the **EN** button in the page header (there is no language control in the demo bar). The language you pick is remembered by the browser, so after a retake the landing page may open in the last language used. Press **EN** on the landing page first.
6. Start signed out, on the landing page. Every part after the first uses **Switch persona** on the demo bar. At phone width the demo bar is folded behind a **Demo** button, so keep the window wide.
7. Know two things before filming: the reading to type in part 3 is `185/115`, and the correct teach-back answer in the app is the second option ("It is a raised reading that needs checking again."). In the thread it is the letter B.
8. Every retake needs a fresh **Reset demo data**, because part 1 changes Khalid's case and part 3 changes Jean-Paul's.
9. Optional cutaway, not in the timing: the "How it works" page (link on the landing page, "How decisions are made").

## Opening (0:00 to 0:15)

Screen: the landing page, title "No raised blood pressure result gets lost", with the loop ring and the eight steps beside it.

Narration: "When a screening finds a raised blood pressure, the result is often issued and then nobody owns the next step. People are not told, referrals stall, and nobody sees it is late. This is Halqa, a prototype that gives every raised result one case, one owner and one due date. Everything you will see is synthetic."
