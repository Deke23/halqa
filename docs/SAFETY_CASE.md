# Halqa: safety case (prototype)

Status on 2026-11-20, after the hardening pass and the localisation pass. This is a hazard log for a hackathon prototype that uses synthetic data only. No clinician has reviewed it. All thresholds, service levels and message text are placeholders pending clinician sign-off. The prototype is not a medical device and has not been assessed against any medical device regulation. It must not be used with real patients. Test names are in `backend/tests/` (hardening tests are in `test_hardening.py`) or `backend/tests_data_ai/`. Residual risk is our own judgement, not a measured value. On 2026-11-20 the automated suite result was: `525 passed, 1 warning in 169.56s (0:02:49)`.

| # | Hazard | How it could happen | Control in the prototype | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Severe reading missed or delayed | Result sits unread. Severe home reading entered later. Patient has warning symptoms but is not advised. The on-call target does not exist as a person | Severe is classified by rule from numbers (`engine/urgency.py`). It flags the case urgent, sends the severe template with red-flag advice, opens a 4 hour clinician task, escalates to the on-call target at the due time and tops the queue (`test_severe_intake_creates_parallel_urgent_review_and_escalates_to_on_call`, `test_severe_intake_lands_first_in_urgent`). Later severe readings flag the case. In the app a red-flag symptom flags it and returns region advice (`test_red_flag_escalates_to_urgent_with_region_advice`). In the SMS thread the red-flag question is asked and a yes answers at once with the emergency instruction, and a severe typed reading gets the urgent template (`test_phone_thread_asks_the_red_flag_question_and_yes_is_urgent_at_once`, `test_severe_sms_reading_gets_the_urgent_template_with_the_emergency_instruction`). That advice bypasses the consent check on purpose and is marked in the audit event | Medium to high. No roster or paging exists, so "on-call" is a label. A patient who never replies is reached only through the reminder steps and a coordinator call. The SMS yes does not say which symptom. The emergency number 998 and all advice text are unchecked by any clinician or official source |
| 2 | Patient does not understand the result | Language, literacy or channel mismatch. Message not read | Plain-language templates in three languages. Teach-back in the app and, now, always in the SMS thread, with a simpler second explanation and a coordinator call task after two wrong answers (`test_wrong_teach_back_does_not_advance_and_second_wrong_creates_call_task`, `test_whole_yaounde_flow_by_replies`). Unreachable protocol with step-specific reminders, a second channel where consented, a call and a clinician decision (`test_unreachable_protocol_ladder`, `test_reminder_wording_matches_the_late_step_and_the_patient_language`). `0` asks for a call in every state | High until reviewed. Arabic and French text has had no native clinical review. The teach-back is a three-option question that can be guessed. Health literacy is not assessed. No real message delivery exists. The reply grammar is rigid |
| 3 | Result communicated as a diagnosis | Template, staff text or re-worded text uses diagnosis wording | Wording is "a raised reading that needs checking" and "not a final result". Blocked diagnosis and alarm words in three languages, checked against our own templates (`test_our_templates_do_not_use_blocked_words`) and against any re-worded text by the validator. Staff cannot send free text: send is by stored draft id and the server re-renders and re-validates (`test_send_refuses_free_text_and_client_supplied_drafts`) | Low to medium. The blocked list is short and a re-wording could still change meaning in ways the validator cannot see. Staff-facing screens use clinical terms |
| 4 | Case lost between providers | Referral not accepted, no owner, clinician leaves, specialist question unanswered | Every case has an owner role and a due date. Rejected and unowned queues. Referral accept and reject with a reason. Owner reassignment gives a fresh due date. Sharing consent checked before referral (`test_referral_reject_reassign_and_rerefer`, `test_withdrawn_sharing_consent_blocks_refer`, `test_seeded_queues_have_the_named_scenarios`) | Medium. Only providers inside the system are tracked. E-consults have no due date or escalation. No real exchange with other systems. Coordinator lead is a label |
| 5 | Case closed wrongly or automatically | A rule, timer or non-clinician closes a case. A clinician closes it without real evidence. A patient who withdraws consent is treated as closed | Only the clinician role can close. An evidence type and a reference or a note of 10 or more characters are required. Nothing in the engine can produce `CLOSED` except `close` (`test_only_close_can_produce_closed`, `test_nothing_closes_a_case_on_its_own`, `test_protocol_exhausted_never_closes`, `test_non_clinicians_cannot_close`, `test_clinician_close_needs_evidence`). Withdrawing care coordination only creates a clinician decision task with a suggested type (`test_withdrawing_care_coordination_stops_automated_messages_and_asks_a_clinician`). Closure and reopen are audited and a clinician can reopen | Medium. The gate checks that a note exists, not that it is sensible. A case waiting on a clinician decision can sit there |
| 6 | AI wording changes a clinical value or adds advice | A model alters a number, date, name or adds a recommendation | Off by default. Re-wording only for staff-requested drafts, from approved template text. Drafts are stored on the server. Send takes only a draft id, the server re-renders the template and re-runs the validator for an AI draft, and refuses it without explicit approval, which is recorded in the audit event (`test_ai_reworded_draft_needs_explicit_approval_and_the_audit_records_it`, `test_ai_reworded_text_is_revalidated_at_send`). The validator checks slot values, new numbers, blocked words, length and language, and falls back to the template on any failure or provider error (`backend/tests_data_ai/test_wording.py`). The engine cannot import wording (`test_engine_imports_nothing_from_wording_or_io`) | Low while off, medium if enabled. The validator checks form, not meaning, and can miss a dropped sentence. Providers were tested only with mocked HTTP. If enabled, patient first name and reading leave the system in the prompt |
| 7 | Data shared without consent | Referral or message sent after the patient withdrew permission. Data shown to the wrong role | One send function checks consent for every outbound message; a withdrawn channel or care coordination suppresses it and writes an event (`test_every_outbound_message_is_stored_by_the_single_send_function`, `test_withdrawn_channel_suppresses_reminders_and_creates_one_task`). STOP withdraws the channel. Withdrawn sharing consent blocks referral. Research use is opt-in and the dashboard counts only opted-in people (`test_dashboard_leaves_out_people_who_have_not_opted_in`). Coordinators get no clinical free text and cannot read the FHIR bundle (`test_coordinator_does_not_get_closure_visit_urgent_or_econsult_notes`, `test_fhir_bundle_is_clinician_only`). Staff reads of a case detail or bundle are audited | High for a real deployment. Two documented bypasses of the channel check (emergency advice answering the patient's own message, and the single STOP confirmation). A patient with no consented channel depends on a coordinator's call. Guest authentication is demo only. Coordinators still see names, readings and messages for their whole region |
| 8 | Small-group re-identification from the dashboard | A split by age, sex or group shows a cell of one or two people | Dashboard is aggregate only. Any cell under 5 people is suppressed in live and simulated outputs. No names, references or initials in responses. Analyst has no case access (`test_dashboard_suppresses_small_cells`, `test_dashboard_is_aggregate_only_and_staff_or_analyst`, `test_small_cell_suppression_applied_everywhere`) | Medium. Simple threshold only: differencing between overlapping splits is not blocked. Demo data is small |
| 9 | Wearable value mistaken for blood pressure | A step count or sleep value, or a wrist-device value, is used as a reading | There is no wearable reading source. A `WEARABLE` source is refused with 422. The diary carries no blood pressure and says so (`test_wearable_is_not_a_blood_pressure_source`, `test_wearable_is_not_a_reading_source`). Home readings count only from a cuff source | Low. No real device is connected. Nothing checks that a home cuff reading was really from a validated cuff, because the patient types it |
| 10 | Patient discouraged by the lifestyle plan | Plan feels like failure or pressure, so the patient disengages | Missed week makes the plan smaller and sends a no-blame message. No streaks, no resets, no red failure state. Larger size offered, never forced. Three missed weeks create a human call task, not a message. Withdrawing the programme stops the plan with no missed week recorded (`test_missed_week_shrinks_and_is_no_blame`, `test_smallest_never_goes_below_smallest_or_resets`, `test_no_penalty_language_in_plan_texts`, `test_withdrawing_lifestyle_programme_stops_a_running_plan`). Plan texts exist in three languages | Medium. Plan content (salt, walking, sleep) has had no clinical or cultural review |
| 11 | Wrong-language or culturally inappropriate message | Poor Arabic or French, wrong register, assumptions about family or lifestyle | Modern Standard Arabic and formal French "vous" aimed for. Blocked words in three languages. Script and language check on re-worded text. The backend supplies names, labels, cover lines and error messages in English, Arabic and French (`backend/tests/test_i18n.py`). Messages are stored and rendered in the patient's language; dates follow the message language; the language switch re-renders from stored slots (`test_a_re_rendered_message_keeps_the_slots_it_was_created_with`) | High until native clinical reviewers check every template and every backend-supplied label. None of the Arabic or French has been reviewed. Dialect and literacy in Yaounde are untested. Arabic SMS and the French red-flag SMS are not GSM-7 safe as recorded in the template files |
| 12 | System unavailable or silently failing | Server down, database error, background task stops | Case state, due dates and the audit log are in the database, so nothing is lost on restart. Evaluation also runs on every case read and clock advance. A failed background pass is logged with a traceback and the loop continues (`test_tick_logs_the_traceback_and_keeps_running`). The production image runs (verified by the team on 18 November 2026) | High for real use. No downtime procedure, monitoring, backup, alerting or load testing. SQLite default. A log line is the only signal of a failed pass. A paper fallback for severe results would be needed in any pilot |
| 13 | Wrong threshold or service level | A placeholder value is wrong for the population, so cases are missed or flooded | One config file with a basis and status for each value, served at `GET /api/config` and labelled placeholder everywhere (`test_every_config_entry_is_a_labelled_placeholder`) | High. No clinician has reviewed any value. Needs sign-off before any pilot |
| 14 | Time zone error in confirmation averages | Home days or the ambulatory daytime window computed in the wrong clock | Days and the daytime window use the patient's region local time (Asia/Dubai, Africa/Douala) (`test_hbpm_days_are_local_days_not_utc_days`, `test_abpm_daytime_window_is_local_time`). The clinician reviews the readings and the engine average is advice only | Low. A patient outside their region's time zone is not handled |
| 15 | Audit log altered or lost | Someone edits or deletes case events to hide a change | ORM guard plus database triggers on SQLite that refuse UPDATE and DELETE, including raw SQL (`test_case_events_are_append_only`, `test_database_refuses_raw_update_and_delete_on_case_events`) | Medium. The PostgreSQL trigger SQL exists but was never run. A database administrator can drop a trigger. The demo reset drops the schema on purpose |
| 16 | Demo controls abused on a shared deployment | Anyone resets data or moves the clock | `DEMO_MODE` switches off guest login, clock, reset and intake simulator. Reset needs staff or analyst (`test_demo_mode_off_disables_every_demo_control`, `test_reset_needs_staff_or_analyst_and_clock_advance_any_signed_in_guest`) | Medium. In demo mode any signed-in user can move the clock. With demo mode off no one can sign in, because real authentication is not built |

## Other limits of this document

This log covers the hazards named above and the extra items found while reading the code. It is not a complete hazard analysis, has not been reviewed by a clinical safety officer, and does not follow a formal clinical safety standard. A clinical safety review is a required step before any pilot.
