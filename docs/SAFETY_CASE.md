# Halqa: safety case (prototype)

Status on 2026-11-18. This is a hazard log for a hackathon prototype that uses synthetic data only. No clinician has reviewed it. All thresholds, service levels and message text are placeholders pending clinician sign-off. The prototype is not a medical device and has not been assessed against any medical device regulation. It must not be used with real patients. Test names are in `backend/tests/` or `backend/tests_data_ai/`. Residual risk is our own judgement, not a measured value.

| # | Hazard | How it could happen | Control in the prototype | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Severe reading missed or delayed | Result sits unread. Severe home reading entered later. Patient has warning symptoms but is not advised. The on-call target does not exist as a person | Severe is classified by rule from numbers (`engine/urgency.py`). It flags the case urgent, sends the severe template with red-flag advice, opens a 4 hour clinician task, escalates to the on-call target at the due time and tops the queue (`test_severe_intake_creates_parallel_urgent_review_and_escalates_to_on_call`, `test_severe_intake_lands_first_in_urgent`). Later severe readings flag the case. Red-flag symptoms flag it and return region advice (`test_red_flag_escalates_to_urgent_with_region_advice`) | High. No roster or paging exists, so "on-call" is a label. In the SMS thread a severe reading from the patient flags the case but the reply is the generic acknowledgement with no emergency advice, and the red-flag question is not asked (found by reading `api/intake.py`, not tested). The emergency number 998 is unchecked |
| 2 | Patient does not understand the result | Language, literacy or channel mismatch. Message not read | Plain-language templates in three languages. Teach-back with a simpler second explanation, and a coordinator call task after two wrong answers (`test_wrong_teach_back_does_not_advance_and_second_wrong_creates_call_task`). Unreachable protocol with reminder, second channel, call and clinician decision (`test_unreachable_protocol_ladder`). Keyword reply "2" asks for a call | High until reviewed. Arabic and French text has had no native clinical review. The teach-back is a three-option question that can be guessed. Health literacy is not assessed. No real message delivery exists |
| 3 | Result communicated as a diagnosis | Template, staff text or re-worded text uses diagnosis wording | Wording is "a raised reading that needs checking" and "not a final result". Blocked diagnosis and alarm words in three languages, checked against our own templates (`test_our_templates_do_not_use_blocked_words`) and against any re-worded text by the validator | Medium. The send endpoint accepts staff text without re-validating it. The blocked list is short. Staff-facing screens use clinical terms |
| 4 | Case lost between providers | Referral not accepted, no owner, clinician leaves, specialist question unanswered | Every case has an owner role and a due date. Rejected and unowned queues. Referral accept and reject with a reason. Owner reassignment gives a fresh due date. Sharing consent checked before referral (`test_referral_reject_reassign_and_rerefer`, `test_withdrawn_sharing_consent_blocks_refer`, `test_seeded_queues_have_the_named_scenarios`) | Medium. Only providers inside the system are tracked. E-consults have no due date or escalation. No real exchange with other systems. Coordinator lead is a label |
| 5 | Case closed wrongly or automatically | A rule, timer or non-clinician closes a case. A clinician closes it without real evidence | Only the clinician role can close. An evidence type and a reference or a note of 10 or more characters are required. Nothing in the engine can produce `CLOSED` except `close` (`test_only_close_can_produce_closed`, `test_nothing_closes_a_case_on_its_own`, `test_protocol_exhausted_never_closes`, `test_non_clinicians_cannot_close`, `test_clinician_close_needs_evidence`). Closure and reopen are audited and a clinician can reopen | Medium. The gate checks that a note exists, not that it is sensible. The unreachable protocol only flags a clinician decision |
| 6 | AI wording changes a clinical value or adds advice | A model alters a number, date, name or adds a recommendation | Off by default. Re-wording only for staff-requested drafts, from approved template text. Validator checks slot values, new numbers, blocked words, length and language, and falls back to the template on any failure or provider error (`backend/tests_data_ai/test_wording.py`). Staff must approve an AI draft before it is sent and the approver is recorded (`test_ai_reworded_draft_needs_approval`). The engine cannot import wording (`test_engine_imports_nothing_from_wording_or_io`) | Medium if enabled, low while off. The validator does not check meaning and can miss a dropped sentence. The send endpoint trusts the client's `source` and text. Providers were tested only with mocked HTTP |
| 7 | Data shared without consent | Referral or message sent after the patient withdrew permission. Data shown to the wrong role | Withdrawn sharing consent blocks referral. Withdrawn channel consent blocks notify and staff send. "STOP" withdraws channel consent. Role and organisation checks on every case read. Staff reads are audited (`test_staff_reads_write_case_viewed_events`, `test_patient_cannot_read_other_cases_or_staff_routes`) | High for a real deployment. `care_coordination` and `research_aggregate` consent are not enforced. Automatic reminders do not check channel consent. A started plan continues after consent is withdrawn. Coordinators see more than intended. Guest authentication is demo only |
| 8 | Small-group re-identification from the dashboard | A split by age, sex or group shows a cell of one or two people | Dashboard is aggregate only. Any cell under 5 people is suppressed in live and simulated outputs. No names, references or initials in responses. Analyst has no case access (`test_dashboard_suppresses_small_cells`, `test_dashboard_is_aggregate_only_and_staff_or_analyst`, `test_small_cell_suppression_applied_everywhere`) | Medium. Simple threshold only: differencing between overlapping splits is not blocked. Demo data is small |
| 9 | Wearable value mistaken for blood pressure | A step count or sleep value, or a wrist-device value, is used as a reading | There is no wearable reading source. A `WEARABLE` source is refused with 422. The diary carries no blood pressure and says so (`test_wearable_is_not_a_blood_pressure_source`, `test_wearable_is_not_a_reading_source`). Home readings count only from a cuff source | Low. No real device is connected. Nothing checks that a home cuff reading was really from a validated cuff, because the patient types it |
| 10 | Patient discouraged by the lifestyle plan | Plan feels like failure or pressure, so the patient disengages | Missed week makes the plan smaller and sends a no-blame message. No streaks, no resets, no red failure state. Larger size offered, never forced. Three missed weeks create a human call task, not a message (`test_missed_week_shrinks_and_is_no_blame`, `test_smallest_never_goes_below_smallest_or_resets`, `test_no_penalty_language_in_plan_texts`) | Medium. Plan content (salt, walking, sleep) has had no clinical or cultural review. Action text in the API is English with label keys |
| 11 | Wrong-language or culturally inappropriate message | Poor Arabic or French, wrong register, assumptions about family or lifestyle | Modern Standard Arabic and formal French "vous" aimed for. Blocked words in three languages. Script and language check on re-worded text. Patient can switch language. Latin digits for clinical values | High until native clinical reviewers check every template. Dialect and literacy in Yaounde are untested |
| 12 | System unavailable or silently failing | Server down, database error, background task stops | Case state, due dates and the audit log are in the database, so nothing is lost on restart. Evaluation also runs on every case read and clock advance, so a stopped task does not stop reads | High for real use. No downtime procedure, monitoring, backup, alerting or load testing. The 60 second task hides all exceptions. SQLite default. A paper fallback for severe results would be needed in any pilot |
| 13 | Wrong threshold or service level | A placeholder value is wrong for the population, so cases are missed or flooded | One config file with a basis and status for each value, served at `GET /api/config` and labelled placeholder everywhere (`test_every_config_entry_is_a_labelled_placeholder`) | High. No clinician has reviewed any value. Needs sign-off before any pilot |
| 14 | Time zone error in confirmation averages | Home days or the ambulatory daytime window computed in UTC, not local time | None beyond the stated assumption. The clinician reviews the readings and the engine average is advice only | Low to medium. A reading near midnight could fall on the wrong day |

## Other limits of this document

This log covers the hazards named above and the extra items found while reading the code. It is not a complete hazard analysis, has not been reviewed by a clinical safety officer, and does not follow a formal clinical safety standard. A clinical safety review is a required step before any pilot.
